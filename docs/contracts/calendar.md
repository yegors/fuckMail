Create/edit `fields` contain title, start, end, all-day flag, IANA time zone, location, description, conference URL and optional attendee email list. Title is nonblank and at most 512 bytes; location 2,048 bytes, description 32,768 bytes, HTTPS conference URL 2,048 bytes and attendees at most 50 addresses. Timed endpoints require offset-bearing RFC3339 values; all-day endpoints use dates and an exclusive end. End must follow start. Unsupported fields and all recurring mutations fail explicitly. RSVP accepts `accepted`, `tentative` or `declined` for the stored self attendee only. Editing an unchanged attendee preserves its self flag and response metadata. The native form retains entered fields when validation, permissions or a stale ETag rejects a save, and keeps the chosen account/calendar identity stable if the catalog changes.

Every projected row retains account/calendar/occurrence identity. Nonrestricted nonrecurring rows additionally expose event identity, ETag, edit/RSVP capabilities and the self response; busy-only rows do not acquire those details during enrichment. Conference URLs and physical location remain separate. Native date/visibility changes clear segments from the previous range before a new reply arrives. An unavailable or stale cache is labeled, and its details cannot be opened as a current snapshot. The calendar and timeline maintain separate scoped request state.

## Calendar

The first milestone implements Google Calendar only. External iCalendar URL subscriptions and their refresh/cache/UI/CLI gates below are explicitly deferred until Google GUI/CLI dogfooding. Google recurrence strings, time zones and invitation semantics may still use iCalendar representations; that necessary parsing is not the deferred subscription feature.

## D038 recurring authoring boundary

Version four adds recurring create, common-rule series edits, occurrence/series self-RSVP and explicit following edit/cancel to the existing conditional journal. Resolve the selected scope and action before editing; series writes target the master, occurrence writes target the verified instance. Common authoring accepts one DAILY/WEEKLY/MONTHLY/YEARLY RRULE, bounded interval/count or a correctly typed UNTIL, and supported weekday/month-day/month selectors. Omission preserves recurrence; an explicit empty series rule clears it. Unsupported rules are preserved for reads and refused where rewriting or splitting would change their meaning.

Following requires explicit confirmation that later exceptions reset. It conditionally trims the original rule and, for an edit, creates a separate deterministic replacement from the selected occurrence. Preserve earlier exceptions and retained source evidence; do not migrate exceptions through a custom framework. Preserve supported fields, guest metadata, labels and attachments. An existing Google Meet becomes a fresh deterministic conference request on the replacement; normal series edits retain the existing meeting. Refuse unrepresentable provider/source semantics before the trim. Both writes use `send_updates:all`; neither acknowledgment nor reconciliation proves notification delivery.

The two steps share one operation and frozen plan. A confirmed trim with an unattempted replacement is `partial`; explicit Resume rechecks current authority and an exact fresh trimmed master before creating the replacement. An uncertain step is never automatically retried. Exact replay only returns its receipt. Retained late trim responses can strengthen that step without overwriting the replacement lease or outcome. Source versions, current membership and lifecycle fences remain authoritative. Paired native/CLI and final release evidence are required separately from focused tests.
